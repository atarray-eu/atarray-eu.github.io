AtlanticArray is a scientific grassroots initiative to deploy a basin-wide network of seismo-acoustic sensors across the Atlantic seafloor over 10–12 years, enabling the first truly holistic, multi-scale imaging of the subsea geosphere—from deep mantle dynamics to surface volcanism—across Earth’s full spectrum of magmatic settings. Through efficient and sustainable data collection, it will also map, for the first time, the Atlantic’s biological, geological, and anthropogenic soundscape. This integrated approach is essential to understand both geodynamic processes and ocean ecosystems, and to track how marine life, seafloor activity, and human noise interact in a rapidly changing ocean—before critical, potentially irreversible changes are missed.
