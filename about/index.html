About

During the EGU 2023–2026 and IUGG 2023 conferences, forum meetings were held to gather the scientific community interested in geophysical studies of the seafloor in the Atlantic region. In addition, the 2024 and 2025 Orfeus and GeoInquire workshops in Helsinki and in Athens included a formal talk and discussion about the AtlanticArray concept, which was also discussed in several presentations (e.g., AGU 2025, AIR centre’s networking fridays, training school in ocean bottom seismology at the 2025 IASPEI meeting, seminars at various institutions worldwide, …). All together, these events included over 600 participants from a large number of different countries and institutes, ranging from academia to national pools of instruments, governmental seismic monitoring agencies and geophysical instrumentation industry. Strong support was received from the scientific community to develop and implement a community-driven AtlanticArray concept aimed at developing a holistic understanding of the Atlantic deep sea and sub-sea system. This led to a dedicated AtlanticArray workshop at University College London (UK) in March 2025 to prepare a community-led white paper on the AtlanticArray concept. Since then, the community has continued its efforts into new partnerships and an implementation plan.
